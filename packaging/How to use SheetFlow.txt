How to use SheetFlow

1. Open the app
   Mac: double-click SheetFlow. The first time, if the Mac blocks it, right-click SheetFlow and choose Open.
   Windows: double-click SheetFlow.cmd and leave that window open.

   Your browser opens the dashboard. There is no sign-in.
   Closing the browser leaves the app running.
   Mac: quit SheetFlow from the Dock.
   Windows: close the SheetFlow window.

2. Import a workbook
   Choose Upload workbook and select an .xls or .xlsx file.
   On Select sheets, uncheck any sheet you want to leave out. Check the header row and the column types.
   On Review, look at the accepted and rejected rows. If some rows were rejected, Continue with accepted rows keeps those rows in the issue list and leaves them out of the dashboard.
   The overview opens when processing finishes.

3. Read the results
   Overview shows accepted rows, rejected rows, and how many sheets were imported.
   Data explorer searches, filters, and exports the rows.
   Validation shows why a row was rejected, with the original value.
   Imports reopens an earlier file, retries a failed import, or deletes one.

Your workbooks and results stay on this computer.
